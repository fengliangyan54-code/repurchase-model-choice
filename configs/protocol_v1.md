# Frozen analysis protocol version 1

Created before accessing test metrics, 2026-10-20. This is an internal specification, not public preregistration.

Question: Can public transaction-only machine learning support prospective repeat-purchase ranking under fixed selection capacity? No treatment or sales-uplift identification is claimed.

Data: UCI Online Retail, DOI 10.24432/C5BW33, official XLSX. Preserve raw bytes and SHA256. Positive purchase lines require a known CustomerID, timestamp, positive quantity and UnitPrice, invoice not beginning C. Remove exact duplicates after counting them. Retain negative/cancelled rows only as descriptive exclusions. Do not silently treat cancellation as churn. Do not trim large valid purchases.

Cohort: customer with >=1 positive purchase in [cutoff-90 days, cutoff). Outcome >=1 positive purchase in [cutoff, cutoff+30 days). Features use only pre-cutoff data; identity is excluded as a predictor. Date range and final outcome completeness are checked against the file. Orders are unique customer-invoice combinations; calendar purchasing days used for BG/NBD.

Training cutoffs 2011-03-01,04-01,05-01,06-01. Validation 2011-07-01. Four untouched final test cutoffs 2011-08-01,09-01,10-01,11-01. Latest training outcomes precede validation; validation outcomes precede test. Training model hyperparameters selected on July macro AP (one cohort), then refit on training+validation with frozen settings. No test-label-based choice or retraining in test. Recurring customers across times are intended for existing-customer CRM; not unseen-customer evaluation.

Features: 90-day recency, unique invoice frequency, gross positive monetary value; extended features: tenure since first observed positive transaction, 30-day frequency, 30-day monetary, unique products90, mean order value90, mean distinct-item count/order90, mean gap between positive purchasing days90 (single-day default90). Positive columns log1p for logistic models then standard scaling, with train-fitted imputation; tree models use raw numeric inputs. No country or CustomerID features.

Baselines: decreasing recency, frequency, monetary, equal-weight rank RFM (within cohort; no outcome used), BG/NBD fit on June cohort for validation and July cohort for test using frequency distinct purchase days-1, recency last-first and T cutoff-first in fractional days. BG/NBD purchase probability derived from alive posterior and Gamma waiting time; all hyperparameters from historical likelihood, penalty candidates .001,.01,.1 chosen by July AP. No aggregate repeat rows treated as independent histories in BTYD fitting.

Supervised: logistic regression RFM-only, logistic extended, random forest extended, histogram gradient boosting extended. Logistic C [.1,1,10]; forest min_samples_leaf [10,30], n_estimators200 max_features .8; HGB max_leaf_nodes[7,15], l2[1,10], max_iter150 learning_rate .05 min_samples_leaf30, early_stoppingFalse; seeds 17,29,43 for forest/HGB development sensitivity, validation selects hyperparameters by mean AP across seeds; seed17 fixed primary report, others supplementary. Equal information and capacity; fitting cost disclosed, no equal-time claim. Primary predeclared family comparison: validation-selected highest-AP supervised model versus validation-selected highest-AP non-ML ranking baseline; both identities frozen before final scoring. Equal-score ties broken by numeric customer ID, not labels.

Primary metric equal-month average precision (AP). Secondary ROC-AUC, Brier (only actual probability models, not rank heuristics), log loss, calibration descriptive, precision/recall/lift at ceil(q*n), q=.1,.2,.3. Primary operational q=.2 specified before evaluation; no profit/ROI interpretation. Gross future revenue coverage is descriptive, highly skewed, not a causal marketing effect and not primary optimization objective.

Uncertainty: paired customer-cluster bootstrap across the four fixed test months, 1000 replications seed20261020; resample customer clusters with replacement, recompute per-month metrics and macro average, use percentile intervals. Ranking and capacity recomputed including cluster multiplicity, ties identical across methods. This is conditional on fitted model and four calendar months, NOT independent-month uncertainty or training-seed uncertainty. Report month-by-month results. Main paired comparison only, other comparisons exploratory; no p-value fishing. AP bootstrap may be costlier than point estimate but do not stop at significance.

Mechanism: same frozen HGB settings on RFM-only; permutation importance on final model/test macro AP, 10 repeats per feature, shuffle within each month, descriptive model reliance, correlated-feature limitation explicit. Training seeds17,29,43 supplementary no selecting best test run.

Correctness: boundary manual case; features/labels separate timestamps; all outcomes complete; positive-only invoices; duplicate ID-cutoff rows absent; deterministic rerun fingerprints; hand-check feature rows. Independent rerun selected model and one metric against saved predictions. Scientific negative results retained. No claim of novel classifier or broad SOTA; only single-retailer historical empirical audit.
