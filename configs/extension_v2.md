# Supplementary cross dataset evaluation specification

Recorded 2026-10-20 after viewing the inherited Online Retail test point estimates and before downloading or inspecting CDNOW transaction records. This is an internal analysis specification, not public preregistration. It supplements frozen_v1 without replacing its primary comparison. CDNOW is a second historical merchant, not a current production deployment or an untouched author-level confirmation experiment.

Source: full CDNOW cohort from https://brucehardie.com/datasets/CDNOW_master.zip, documented at https://brucehardie.com/notes/026/. Use the complete cohort rather than the outcome-stratified one-tenth sample. Record retrieval UTC, exact bytes and SHA256. Retain original archive locally; no assumed redistribution license. Include retrieval instructions in the deliverable rather than redistributing its raw records.

Observation unit is a recorded transaction, with customer ID, date, quantity and spend. There is no invoice ID or product identifier. Keep repeated identical records because separate orders cannot be distinguished; report their count and run a deduplication sensitivity. Require parsed dates, known IDs, positive quantity and spend, logging exclusions. Do not merge this merchant with UCI or pool currency amounts.

Eligibility and target retain the 90-day history and next-30-day positive-purchase definitions. Training cutoffs: April to July 1997. Validation cutoff: August 1997, retained only for descriptive checking; no hyperparameter or model choice. Test cutoffs: September 1997 through June 1998, all ten reported, with the full 30-day June outcome ending July 1. Source documentation states observation through end June; verify the actual maximum date. Fit supervised models on April-August rows, once. Fit BG/NBD once on August's eligible customer histories. No refitting during test.

Use frozen_v1 hyperparameters and seed17 for every model; do not choose a winner using CDNOW. Evaluate recency, frequency, monetary, equal-rank RFM, BG/NBD, LR_RFM, RF_RFM, HGB_RFM, LR_common, RF_common, HGB_common. RFM frequency counts recorded transactions; BG/NBD counts distinct purchase days. Common features are recency90, frequency90, monetary90, tenure, frequency30, monetary30, avg_order90, mean_gap90. Product counts are unavailable and are omitted, not fabricated. Dollar values are used only within CDNOW; no cross-currency pooling or direct reuse of fitted UCI coefficients. This is a transport of the evaluation approach and frozen settings with merchant-specific fitting, not zero-shot transfer.

Report all 110 model-month cells and equal-month AP, AUC, Brier, log loss and capacity measures. Supplementary comparison is RF_RFM minus BG/NBD, 1000 customer-cluster paired bootstrap replicates with seed20261021. It is exploratory relative to the inherited project; no pooled cross-merchant significance claim or multiplicity-adjusted superiority claim. Retain failures. Run the same settings after exact-record deduplication as sensitivity and report all methods; no selecting between data policies based on results.

Supplementary UCI checks: independently recompute saved AP and capacity metrics without core.evaluate, trace selected feature rows back to raw positive transactions, and fit BG/NBD on all observed customers at July 1 rather than only recently eligible customers, using the same penalty. The latter is a post hoc implementation robustness check, not a replacement comparator. Preserve original run fingerprints.
